# Commerce

Checkout is available for completed appointments. It creates one active invoice whose items copy appointment-service snapshots. The server calculates subtotal, discount, configured-rate tax, tip, total, and balance using integer minor units.

Manual payments represent money collected outside Pawsh. They may not exceed the outstanding balance. They change the invoice from open to partially paid or paid in the same transaction. Corrections are represented by audited void/reversal records; Pawsh does not claim an external refund.

# Pawsh MVP

Pawsh serves a single-location grooming salon with roughly 2–10 team members. Its complete operational loop is:

Customer → Pet → Appointment → Check-in → Service → Completion → Checkout → Manual payment record → History

The MVP includes owner-managed permissions, tenant isolation, employees without required login identities, service snapshots, conflict-safe scheduling, pet safety context, separate operational and financial states, receipts, transactional events, basic email intent handling, audit history, and a daily dashboard.

Native apps, customer self-booking, live card processing, multi-location workflows, inventory, payroll, marketing, advanced analytics, and AI are non-goals.

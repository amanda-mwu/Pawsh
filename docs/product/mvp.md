# Pawsh MVP

Pawsh serves a single-location grooming salon with roughly 2–10 team members. Its complete operational loop is:

Customer → Pet → Appointment → Check-in → Service → Completion → Checkout → Manual payment record → History

The MVP includes owner-managed permissions, tenant isolation, employees without required login identities, service snapshots, conflict-safe scheduling, pet safety context, separate operational and financial states, receipts, transactional events, basic email intent handling, audit history, and a daily dashboard.

Native apps, customer self-booking, live card processing, multi-location workflows, inventory, payroll, marketing, advanced analytics, and AI are non-goals.

Payments in the MVP mean recording supported manual or offline payments. Customer
reminders are transactional appointment reminders, not marketing automation, and
document upload means the specialized rabies-PDF workflow rather than generic
attachments.

Pawsh has completed the working-product stage and is in controlled-pilot
hardening. The [product roadmap](roadmap.md) is authoritative for lifecycle,
future product sequencing, conditional candidates, and approval status. It does
not replace validation evidence or release gates.

The controlled-pilot extension for structured manual rabies information,
appointment-date evaluation, and optional supporting evidence is defined in the
[rabies compliance contract](rabies-appointment-compliance.md).
